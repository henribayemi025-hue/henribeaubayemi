# Léo : prompt A pour Gemini (200 idées), 27/10

Demande de Beau : même démarche que Learn, mais pour Léo. Seulement l'essentiel : code, chat, messagerie, liens, connecteurs. Pas d'organigramme, pas de jeux. Et un écran avec presque pas de texte : pas de titres inutiles, pas de paragraphes d'explication (règle « moins de texte dans Léo », CLAUDE.md §12).

```
You are a world-class product designer and AI-tools strategist. I am building "Léo": a workspace where a company's AI agents do real work for people. You talk to the agents in a chat, they write and run code, read and send messages, use connected tools and links, and they show a decision or a conclusion to approve instead of asking you to write prompts. It is a worldwide product for any company.

WHAT LÉO IS ABOUT (essentials only)
- Chat with agents: the main surface.
- Agents that write and run code.
- Messaging: agents read, draft and send messages; a human approves anything irreversible, external or involving money.
- Links and connectors: email, calendar, files, spreadsheets, social tools and others; agents can use them.
- Tasks: each agent has open tasks, works through them, and the human reviews the result.
- A "Confirm" card before any risky action.
- Every number shown comes from a deterministic tool, never from the model's own arithmetic, and cites the tool that produced it.
- Vertical workflows plugged into a company's own data, not a generic assistant; the system acts in the background and shows a conclusion to validate.

OUT OF SCOPE FOR THIS ROUND (do not propose): organisation charts, buildings or floor plans, games, gamification, learning content.

THE BIG DESIGN RULE
Almost no text. The current interface says too much and has too many titles. Every screen says ONE thing: one short title at most (or none), one main action, and the numbers that matter. Explanations are folded away behind a small "how it works" control or removed. No paragraphs under headings. Icons, spacing, motion and typography carry the meaning. Treat every word as a cost.

YOUR TASK
Give me 200 NEW, specific ideas to make Léo the most loved and trusted way for a company to work with AI agents. Each idea: a short name, one sentence on what it does, one on why a person would love it. About 20 per group:
1. Visual wow with almost no text (the newest UI patterns of 2026: pop-ups, sheets, hover cards, toasts, micro-interactions)
2. Chat (how a conversation with agents should feel, look and be organised)
3. Code (agents writing, running and showing code and results)
4. Messaging (inbox, drafts, approvals, threads, tone)
5. Links and connectors (connecting, permissions, status, safety, discovery)
6. Tasks and approvals ("Confirm" cards, review, undo, audit trail)
7. Trust and numbers (showing where a figure comes from, checks that block a wrong display, honest failure states)
8. Agents (roles, presence, hand-offs, how they show what they are doing)
9. Phone, speed and accessibility
10. Delight (small details that make people smile, without gimmicks)

RULES
- Specific enough that a developer could start building it tomorrow. No generic filler.
- Nothing based on invented statistics or fake social proof.
- Worldwide product: do not tie it to any one country.
- Put your 20 best ideas at the very top, ranked, before the 10 groups.
Number the ideas 1 to 200.
```
